# ArUco Extrude: the 3D-printing features to bring back (outline, 2026-10-20)

Zach is lifting the 3D pause of CF-13 (2026-10-17: "3D printed multi material too unreliable for now… keep the 3D things as placeholders"). This note lists what a 3D-printed marker needs, what the core already does, what the facade (`aruco_studio/web_api.py`, `web/src/core/contract.ts`) and the web UI expose today, and a recommended v1 cut. The five UI proposals are in the babble gallery `reports/media/3d-proposals-2026-10-20.html` (gitignored). The AI default there is V1 Section Strip (84/100), with V4 Preset Recipes next (76).

> “I also want to start adding back in the 3D features for 3D printed markers. Please call that feature Aruco Extrude, not just Aruco (A for Aruco Extrude). ... Within aruco studio I don't think we need to support that much. Basically it's about being able to: 1. the label font should be one that is easy to 3D print; 2. you can choose to extrude just white or black; 3. you can control the white height and the black height; 4. values automatically set by preset, using information like nozzle width, print height etc. Of course you can always override it.”
>
> Zach, 2026-10-20 (`Sources/Daily Notes/Daily Note - Oct 20 2026.md`)

**Naming.** The feature is **ArUco Extrude** (key A). `main` still labels the tool "ArUco" (`web/src/app/Toolbar.tsx:31`). At the time of writing, an uncommitted edit in the main checkout renames it to "ArUco Extrude". That edit isn't this lane's.

## 1. What the core already does for 3D

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Colour modes | `core/slabs.py` `MODES = ("both", "black", "white")`; `core/presets.py` adds `"auto"` | **Both**: a white tile with the black inlaid. **Black only**: black cells into the body under it. **White only**: the white pattern into a dark body. **Auto**: Black on a face, Both in open space. The facade's own note: "mode auto: black (a body under it)" / "mode auto: both (nothing under it)". |
| Heights | `slabs.default_numbers`, `NUMBER_KEYS` | Extrude's words: each colour has an **Offset** and a **Height**, and both can be negative (Zach, 2026-10-08). Defaults: a white tile [0, 2.0] mm with the black 0.6 mm down from its top, i.e. [1.4, 2.0]. Black only: [-0.6, 0]. |
| Flush / raised / recessed / floating | `slabs.plan` notes | All are allowed, each with a one-line note ("the black is recessed 0.20 mm below the white top"). The pocket and recess cut tools follow from the slabs. |
| Locating pins, Operation, Trim excess, Corner cut | `slabs.pin_outlines`, `OPS`, `core/trim.py`, `core/chamfer.py` | Triangle or round pins under a free tile with matching holes in the body; Join vs New body; trim to the body; a 45° corner cut. |
| Layers with z | `plan/layers.py`, `plan/mesh.py` | The bodies in build order, with z along the face normal. `mesh.stl` writes one STL per body. |
| Pixel label font | `core/label.py` (`GLYPH_ROWS = 5`, `STROKE_NOZZLE = 1.0`, `LINE_RULE`) | A blocky font whose stroke and every gap are at least one line wide, and whose cap is 5 strokes. Default: 3.375 mm = 5 × 0.675. Aruco profile: 3.4 mm = 5 × 0.68. |
| Cells in whole lines | `core/autosize.py`, `presets.cell_setting` | Under a PrusaSlicer preset every cell is a whole number of line widths: "cells 6.12 mm = 9 × 0.68 mm". Default: the legacy 0.5 mm marker steps. |
| Print presets + PrusaSlicer sync | `core/presets.py`, `core/prusa.py` | A preset is `profile` (nozzle, line, layer, first layer) + `cells` + `tag` (mode, op, heights, pins) + `label`. A PrusaSlicer print profile with "Aruco" in its name becomes a built-in, read only ("nozzle 0.6, lines 0.68, layers 0.32 (first 0.2), arachne"). |
| Whole-layer check | `presets.layer_note` | "black 0.60 mm face down = the 0.2 mm first layer + 1.25 × 0.32 mm: 0.52 mm or 0.84 mm prints whole layers". It's a note only; nothing is snapped. |
| Beds, orientation, 3MF | `core/beds.py` | Each part is laid face down when flush (face up when raised or recessed). Parts are packed on beds with the wipe tower kept clear, and written as one PrusaSlicer `.3mf` with the MMU colours assigned. |
| Slice check | `verify/slice_check.py` | A headless PrusaSlicer slice checked against the plan: layers, the black's heights, lines per cell, ink IoU. Tests only. |
| Fusion | `adapters/recipe.py`, `adapters/fusion/bodies.py` | A TagPlan becomes Fusion bodies, part by part, from the plan's layers. |

**Found while building the fixture: White only drops the label.** `cad_layers` puts `label_ink` at the black layer's z. White only has no black layer, so the label never reaches `layers`, even though `plan.label.polygons` holds its 18 pixel boxes. That's a facade gap, and the fix is small (in White only the label is white, cut like the pattern).

## 4. What he may be forgetting

| Item | Why it matters | Core | Facade | UI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Print orientation** (face down / up) | Face down, the pattern prints on the bed, so the cells should be whole **first-layer** widths (CF-4) and the colour change happens in the first layers. | Has it: `beds.choose_orientation` picks face down when flush. **New**: the cells follow the first-layer width; today they use `line_mm`. | Gap (`first_line_mm` unused) | Gap |
| **Flush vs raised vs recessed; Flip with no reference face** (CF-8) | Flush is what prints cleanly face down. Raised or recessed changes the orientation and what the camera sees. Flip decides which side of a free tile is the black. | Has all of it | Flip has it; offsets are a gap | Flip has it. Offsets are deliberately hidden (flush only). |
| **Quiet zone / white border** | In Black only the white border is the *body's* plastic. A dark body under a Black only tag has no quiet zone. | Has `quiet_modules` | Has it | Gap (not shown); **new**: a note when Black only sits on a body of unknown colour |
| **Colour order: the filament change layer** | Without an MMU (CF-13's reliability problem), one manual filament change is the dependable path, but it can't print the default flush inlay: flush puts both colours in the same layers (layers 1–2 face down), which needs the MMU. One swap works only **face up with the black raised**: the white tile, swap at 1.80 mm, then the black cells on top. The core plans that today (black [1.8, 2.32], no pocket, Joint Origin at 2.32 mm), so lifting the pause means choosing between CF-8 (flush only) and a single swap. | Raised is planned; **new**: the swap height as an output (`beds` writes MMU extruder slots only) | Gap | Gap. An info line in v1 that says which: "MMU: 2 colours in layers 1–2" or "1 swap at 1.80 mm, face up"; the G-code colour change later. |
| **Bed-size lint** (CF-6) | A tag bigger than the bed should be flagged red but still allowed, like the page lint. | `beds` rejects an oversize part at packing time only | Gap (`bed_mm` ignored) | Gap |
| **A 3D preview of the heights** | Heights are invisible on a 2D canvas, so you can't see Both vs Black only or a recess. | `layers` z, `mesh.bodies` | Has `layers` | Gap (the old Studio's View3D could be reused) |
| **Export: STL / 3MF per colour, or the PrusaSlicer hand-off** | The output of a 3D marker is a mesh per colour, not a paper sheet. | Has `mesh.stl`, `beds` 3MF; `server/prusa_launch.py` is server only | Gap (no call in `API`) | Gap |
| **Verify of a 3D print: the filament contrast** | The detector needs dark against light. Thin white PLA over black shows grey, and glossy black glares. | **New**. `verify/render.py` draws ideal greys (0 / 255, and assumes the body under a Black only tag is white); `slice_check` checks the geometry, not the colour. | Gap | Gap. A note in v1 ("white ≥ 4 layers over black"). |
| **Joint Origin z** | The CAD frame must sit on the printed surface: the tile top (2.0 / 1.8 mm) on a free tag, 0 on a face. | Has it | Has it (`origin_z_mm`) | Shown in 2D only; the z isn't labelled. |

| The default for new tags | `NEW_TAG_PRESET` is inkjet since CF-13. Lifting CF-13 doesn't change that by itself. | Has it | — | Decide whether a 3D preset becomes a built-in choice again (it is listed: "Default") |

## 5. Recommended v1 cut

Smallest thing that covers Zach's four asks and keeps Fusion parity:

1. **Contract.** `PlanRequest.extrude?: { mode: "auto" | "both" | "black" | "white"; white_mm?: number; black_mm?: number }`. Absent means today's plan byte for byte. Heights only: flush, no offsets, no pins, no Operation.
2. **Facade.** `spec_of` maps `mode` → `settings.mode`, `white_mm` → `numbers.white_height`, and `black_mm` → `numbers.black_height` (negative, from the white top). The label ink goes into White only. `layer_note` comes back as an `info` issue on the height field.
3. **Preset-driven heights (new, versioned rule).** With `layer_mm` and `first_layer_mm` set: black = first + k × layer ≥ 0.5 mm (Aruco: 0.52 mm), white = first + n × layer ≥ 1.8 mm (Aruco: 1.80 mm). With them unset (Default): 0.6 / 2.0 as today. Shown grey, with ↺ when typed.
4. **UI (Make tab).** Extrude: Black \| White \| Both (Auto driven), White height, Black depth, and one picture of the layer stack. The toolbar reads "ArUco Extrude" (A).
5. **Export.** One STL per colour from `mesh.stl` through a new `web_api` call. The 3MF / PrusaSlicer hand-off comes later.
6. **Info lines only:** how the colours print (flush = the MMU, two colours in layers 1–2; raised = one swap at the white top, face up) and the colour contrast. **Zach decides:** flush only (CF-8, needs the MMU) vs allowing raised black for single-swap printers. Default if he says nothing: flush stays the default, raised waits.

**Later:** first-layer cell sizing, the bed lint, a 3D orbit view, the 3MF with the colour change, pins / Operation / offsets in the web, and the filament contrast in Verify.
