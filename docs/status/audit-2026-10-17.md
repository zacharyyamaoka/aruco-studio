## Not fixed, and why

| Id | Why |
|---|---|
| P3 (A3 of the first list) | Zach decides: a label cut out of the black with a stroke under one line (0.64 mm against 0.68 mm) loses font pixels in the slice. Either a new label rule (now **rule 5**, since rule 4 went to FB-13 above: a cut-from-black stroke is at least one line width) or a finer black nozzle. The strict xfail stays and now slices the forearm as M8c did (`'#id + name'`, shared ids, fixture `forearm_named`): 37 of 216 pixels under 60 %, as before. The shipped forearm's shorter `'#0-5'` prints |
| B1 | Live re-placement on the forearm is still slow. Measured now (TestClient, the forearm's body 150-157 mm long, median of 8): 2D preview (`top=1`) 245 ms (327 ms with the old example), 3D (`top=1&mesh=1`) 715 ms (1,031 ms). Plans are cached per spec already, and only the two long faces change; the time is the top view and the mesh (`plan/mesh.py`, 20-100 ms per tag). The fix is a design change: plan the faces in a process pool, or a light drag preview (outline and layout) with the meshes on release. Recorded, not started |
| B2 (part) | the dialog still opens on PrusaSlicer's current printer, as M8b2.3 requires; it does not preselect the matching printer. The line names the matching printers and a click picks one. No "Apply ontario's preset to the tags" button: the Preset row's pick does that |
| B3 | a sticker's ChArUco Auto still uses the 3D tube rule (k squares across, raised to the printable minimum), not the largest board that fits. Changing it changes Auto for every stored sticker, so it needs a new versioned rule (make rule 2 or an autosize rule) and Zach's word on "fills" (2 × 2 of 13.5 mm vs 3 × 3 of 9 mm). Large; left for a lane |
