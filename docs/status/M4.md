# M4 status: the Fusion adapter skeleton

Built 2026-10-16 on `main` in ROOT, beside the M3 (Studio) agent on other paths. Written after the post-M3 audit (finding C6), from the M4 commits and `docs/FUSION_ADAPTER.md`. Nothing here ran in Fusion.

## In one paragraph

`aruco_studio/adapters/` turns a `TagPlan` into Fusion bodies, sketches and one Joint Origin per tag. `recipe.py` is host-neutral (a plan as build steps); `fusion/` is the add-in's custom feature: create, compute, edit, restructure, sync, records, ids and paint. It runs only against a recording stand-in of `adsk` (`tests/adapters/fake_adsk.py`): every `evaluate()` golden case builds, reads back and edits there. Switching Zach's live add-in to it is the Fusion-gated part (gates F1-F8), not done.
